Who We Are

A single home for the rental market

KPR is an online property platform built to help people search, list, and manage rentals without the back-and-forth. We serve tenants looking for a place to live, and owners looking for tenants they can rely on.

Our Mission

Faster, safer, simpler renting

We remove the friction from renting through verified listings, secure messaging, and clear booking steps — so both sides can move forward with confidence.

Our Vision

A market people actually trust

We're working toward a rental market where every listing is verified, every payment is secure, and finding a good home is never left to chance.

Where We Are Today

1,200+ properties, 5,000+ tenants matched

With 98% of owners verified, KPR has become one of the fastest-growing rental platforms across Kampu.
